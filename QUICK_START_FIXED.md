## QUICK TUTORIAL

### 1. Change Settings
- **Boundary Type**: Circle, Square, or Triangle
- **Object Shape**: sphere, cube, star, or triangle
- **Max Objects**: 5-200
- **Speed**: Adjust speed slider

### 2. Click Play
- Objects start bouncing
- They collide and create more objects
- Speed gradually increases

### 3. Watch the Build-up
- Status shows "Objects: X/50"
- Particles multiply as they collide
- FPS stays at 60

### 4. At Max Objects
- All 50 objects bouncing
- Status shows "Objects: 50/50"
- Animation continues smoothly

### 5. Calm Down (Optional)
- After 20 seconds: "Calm down in 19.8s"
- Motion gradually slows
- Beautiful graceful ending

### 6. Try New Settings
- Click "Apply New Settings" (green button)
- Instantly restarts with new shape/boundary
- No need to click Play again!
